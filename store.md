# DDB Gamelog

Rolls made on D&D Beyond show up in your Owlbear Rodeo room, as a roll list and as cards on the map, for everyone in the room. **The GM sets it up once; players install nothing.**

![A room with the Gamelog roll list open and a roll card on the map](https://iamwarhead.github.io/ddb-game-log-for-obr/assets/img/home-overview.png)

DDB Gamelog is not a new project. It has been bringing D&D Beyond rolls to virtual tabletops for almost six years, and this extension builds on that experience.

## How it works

1. The GM adds the extension, switches it on in the room and connects D&D Beyond once.
2. Anyone rolls on dndbeyond.com or in the D&D Beyond player app.
3. The roll appears in the room's roll list and as a card in a corner of the map.

Players need no account, no extension and no membership. Rolls that someone sent to the DM or kept to themselves on D&D Beyond stay private.

**Your login stays with you.** Your D&D Beyond cookie is stored only in the GM's browser. It is never saved on our server and never put in the room, so your players can't see it.

## What it does

- **Roll list and map cards (Free):** every roll appears with its dice, formula and total. You see pending "rolling…" cards, kept and dropped dice, and advantage, disadvantage, critical hit and fumble tags. The GM chooses the corner, offset and fade out of the cards, whether players see the history, and whether rolls survive a reload.
- **Roll privacy (Free):** D&D Beyond rolls sent to the DM or kept to yourself stay in the GM's roll list. Only public rolls reach the players and Discord.
- **Initiative (Free):** an initiative roll sets the token's initiative in Owlbear Rodeo's Initiative Tracker. Choose to ask before replacing an existing count, and add an optional Dexterity tiebreaker. Reading Dexterity from D&D Beyond is PRO.
- **Character linking (Free):** link tokens to D&D Beyond characters in a settings table or from the token menu, so rolls, initiative and stats land on the right token. The first initiative roll can link a token for you.
- **Party import (PRO):** import your D&D Beyond party with one click. Tokens are linked by name, and missing characters are added to your Owlbear Rodeo Characters panel with their D&D Beyond avatar, ready to place on the map.
- **Live token stats (PRO):** hit points, maximum hit points, temporary hit points, armor class and name tags follow D&D Beyond through [Stat Bubbles for D&D](https://extensions.owlbear.rodeo/bubble-tracker). You pick which stats are written.
- **Card look (Basic):** eight card themes, including D&D Beyond light and dark, and the character's D&D Beyond avatar on each card.
- **Statistics:** a free teaser of the session so far. Basic adds the party at a glance, highlights and a table per character. PRO adds monsters, checks and saves, combat, luck, awards, charts and the session history.
- **Session recap (PRO):** a recap of the session as a card in the roll list and as a message in Discord, also automatically when a session ends.
- **Import and export (PRO):** read earlier rolls back from D&D Beyond so the statistics reach before tracking began, and export the figures as CSV or JSON.
- **Discord (PRO):** public rolls, posted as the character with the dice, and the session recap in your channel.
- **More rolls (PRO):** monster rolls, rolls from the D&D Beyond mobile app, Avrae rolls and rolls from other Owlbear extensions.
- **Several GMs:** only one GM browser relays rolls at a time, and another GM can take over from the settings.
- **Legendary Patrons (MAX):** MAX supporters can put their name on the extension's Legendary Patrons page.

## Works with your other extensions

- **Official [Initiative Tracker](https://extensions.owlbear.rodeo/initiative-tracker) (Free):** D&D Beyond initiative rolls set the initiative of your linked tokens, with an optional Dexterity tiebreaker.
- **[Stat Bubbles for D&D](https://extensions.owlbear.rodeo/bubble-tracker) (PRO):** hit points and armor class of linked tokens follow D&D Beyond, shown as the health bars and bubbles you already use.
