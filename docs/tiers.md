# Tiers

Gamelog features depend on your Patreon membership.

| Tier | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Free | $0 |
| Gamelog Basic | $3 |
| Gamelog PRO | $5 |
| Gamelog MAX | $15 |

Each tier includes everything in the tiers below it. Gamelog MAX has the same features as Gamelog PRO; what you get on top of them is priority support, Discord roles and early access to new builds.

The membership is the one of the GM whose browser relays D&D Beyond rolls, and it applies to **everyone in the room**. Players need no membership. The same Patreon account also covers your Foundry worlds.

## Get a membership

Join on [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/iamwarhead/).

Then link Patreon to your room on the Connection page, see [First steps]({{ '/first-steps.html' | relative_url }}#link-patreon). Changes on Patreon apply within seconds.

{: .note }
That means within seconds of **Patreon** making the change, which is not always the moment you click. An upgrade is charged and takes effect at once. A downgrade or a cancellation only takes effect when the period you already paid for ends, so you keep the higher tier until then, that is Patreon's doing, not Gamelog's. Never change your real tier just to try something: use a second, free Patreon account.

## Product keys

Most people never need one. A product key is an alternative to Patreon: a code handed out for a bundle, a gift or a special case, which grants a tier without a Patreon account.

If you received one, paste it into **Product key (optional)** under **Membership** on the **Connection** page and click **Use**. The key is kept in this browser only, so a second GM browser needs its own copy. The Connection page then shows your membership as "via product key". Leave the field empty to keep the key that is already stored. The page tells you when a key is not valid, has expired or was revoked.

Having both a key and a Patreon link is fine: the higher of the two is what you get.

## If another GM relays

Features follow the membership of the GM who relays. If another GM takes over, their membership decides what the room gets. A card theme set by the first GM only shows when the new relaying GM's membership includes it, see [Only one GM relays]({{ '/troubleshooting.html' | relative_url }}#relaying-gm).
