# For players

If you play in a room where the GM uses DDB Gamelog, you do not need to do anything. There is nothing to install, no account to make and no membership to buy.

## What you see

- The **Gamelog button** in the toolbar opens the **roll list**: the latest rolls of the room, with the dice and the total.
- **Roll cards** appear in a corner of your map when someone rolls. They fade out by themselves, or you can dismiss them with **✕**, or **Clear all**.
- Cards can have a theme and a character avatar, if the GM's membership includes them, see [Card look]({{ '/features/card-look.html' | relative_url }}).
- Initiative rolls can show the roller's Dexterity, if the GM switched that on.

{% include screenshot.html src="player-roll-list.png" alt="The Gamelog window as a player: the roll list without a Settings button" %}

You see **public** rolls only. Rolls that someone sent to the DM or kept to themselves on D&D Beyond are not shown to you in Owlbear Rodeo, see [Who sees a roll]({{ '/features/rolls-and-roll-cards.html#who-sees-a-roll-free' | relative_url }}).

## What you do not get

- **No settings.** Only the GM has the Settings button. The GM's choices apply to the whole room.
- **No statistics.** The statistics page is for the GM.
- **Rolls while no GM is connected.** Rolls reach the room through the GM's browser. If no GM has D&D Beyond connected in the room, no new rolls arrive; ask your GM to check, see [Troubleshooting]({{ '/troubleshooting.html' | relative_url }}#rolls-do-not-arrive).

## If the GM does not share the history

The GM can switch the roll history off for players. Your Gamelog window then shrinks to a note, "The GM does not share the roll history.", and you get no earlier rolls when you join. Live roll cards still appear on your map.

The GM switches it with **Players see the roll history** under **Settings → Rolls & cards → Roll history**.

## Your own rolls

Roll on D&D Beyond, as usual. The roll shows up in the room a moment later, with a pending "Rolling…" card first. Your character should be linked to a token by the GM for initiative to work, see [Character linking]({{ '/features/character-linking.html' | relative_url }}).
