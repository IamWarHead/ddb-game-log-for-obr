# DDB Gamelog for Owlbear Rodeo

DDB Gamelog brings your D&D Beyond game log into [Owlbear Rodeo](https://www.owlbear.rodeo/) (OBR). Rolls made on D&D Beyond show up in a roll list and as cards on the map, for everyone in the room. **The GM sets it up once; players install nothing.**

You are an Owlbear Rodeo GM or a player in a GM's room; you do not need to know anything about code.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Owlbear Rodeo | A free Owlbear Rodeo account for the GM |
| D&D Beyond | The GM is the DM of the campaign that is connected |
| Players | Nothing to install, no account on Gamelog |
| Status | Stable release: 1.0.0 |

{% include screenshot.html src="home-overview.png" alt="An Owlbear Rodeo room with the Gamelog roll list open and a roll card on the map" %}

{: .note }
The extension and the [Foundry VTT module](https://iamwarhead.github.io/ddb-game-log/) share one Gamelog server and one Patreon membership. A membership you already have works here too, see [Tiers]({{ '/tiers.html' | relative_url }}).

## Get started

1. Make sure you meet the [requirements]({{ '/install.html' | relative_url }}#requirements).
2. [Install the extension]({{ '/install.html' | relative_url }}) and switch it on in your room.
3. Follow [First steps]({{ '/first-steps.html' | relative_url }}) to connect D&D Beyond and pick your campaign.
4. Roll something on D&D Beyond and look at the roll list in Owlbear Rodeo.
